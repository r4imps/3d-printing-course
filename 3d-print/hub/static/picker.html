How it works

Drive: click a cell; the cup goes up to travel Z, across, and down to hover Z, never into a pocket. Edit layout: drag a holder to move it, drag over empty cells to select, then add a holder. Test: teach the points and run the glass cycle; results go to docs/refit_test.csv. STOP and speed are in the hub bar.
